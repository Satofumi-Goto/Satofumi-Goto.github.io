Autonomous Mobility Business Development

Independent work & prototypes

Autonomous mobility, designed for viable operations.

I develop new mobility businesses that turn autonomous driving and AI into customer value, connecting vehicles, services, and infrastructure and bringing together the expertise and partnerships needed to move from concept to real-world implementation.

Profile

I joined DENSO in 1997, working in automotive sales, and have been involved in business development in the CASE and MaaS domains since 2013.

At Honda, I currently work in next-generation business development, providing executive leadership with information for investment decisions and formulating strategies. Within this role, I am advancing FCAD-MaaS business development, examining autonomous-driving operations platforms, MaaS vehicles, and service hub development as an integrated whole.

The scope of value creation is expanding from the vehicle itself to entire operations and services enabled by AI. My next focus is to connect autonomous driving with the AI-driven industrial revolution across society, linking diverse industries and services with mobility at the center.

My strength is solving structural problems by working across domains. I translate a wide range of impacts and operating conditions into system requirements, connecting system-wide optimization with implementation.

I aim to combine my experience with AI technologies and expertise across disciplines, taking an integrated approach to vehicles, operations, and services from concept through implementation. By transforming services through AI agents, I seek to create new customer value through autonomous driving.

Application Prototypes

01 / Customer experience Seneschal Seneschal combines the one-stop convenience of MaaS with Activity-based Mobility, which organizes transportation around activities. It fills functional gaps that API integration alone cannot address, enabling users to compare activities, services, and transportation as complete options. It provides a consistent experience across delegating arrangements to AI, making choices and adjustments themselves, and checking the status of their services and journeys. Explore prototype ↗ 02 / Operations system Federation Federation concretizes the “Industrial Revolution through AI utilization” as an operational foundation in which multiple AIs and systems work collaboratively. It is an integrated platform designed to comprehensively understand interdependencies and supply-demand and resource constraints that cannot be fully captured through individual automation, and to handle everything from detecting operational failures and their early signs to analyzing causes, coordinating responses, and restoring operations.
